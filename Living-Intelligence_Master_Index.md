🧭 What Is Living Intelligence?

Living Intelligence is not just AI theory. Living Intelligence is not just biology. Living Intelligence is not just philosophy of mind.

Living Intelligence is the view that intelligence is a living, evolving, relational process that spans biology, technology, culture, and cosmos.

It represents:

Intelligence as process, not product

Minds as ecologies, not machines

Learning as growth, not optimization


Core principle:

> Intelligence is something that lives, not something that runs.
